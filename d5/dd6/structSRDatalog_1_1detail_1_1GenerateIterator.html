template<mir::CColumnSource Source, typename IteratorPosMap, typename IteratorTuple>
struct SRDatalog::detail::GenerateIterator< Source, IteratorPosMap, IteratorTuple >

Template metaprogramming helper to get an iterator from a ColumnSource using pre-created iterator tuple.

Uses the IteratorPosMap to look up the iterator from a pre-created iterator tuple. The iterators are updated in-place during pipeline execution as variables are bound, so no prefix computation is needed here.
