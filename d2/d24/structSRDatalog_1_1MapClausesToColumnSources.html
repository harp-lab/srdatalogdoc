Detailed Description

template<CVar VarToBind, tmp::CTuple NormalClauses, tmp::CTuple BoundVars, tmp::CTuple JoinPlan>
struct SRDatalog::MapClausesToColumnSources< VarToBind, NormalClauses, BoundVars, JoinPlan >

Maps multiple normal clauses to ColumnSource MIR operations.

Converts a list of normal clauses into their corresponding ColumnSource operations, all in the context of the same bound variables and join plan.
