template<CVar Var, tmp::CTuple BodyClauses>
struct SRDatalog::FindLetClauses< Var, BodyClauses >

Finds all LetClauses in a body that bind a specific variable.

Filters the body clauses to find all LetClauses that introduce the specified variable. Used during MIR compilation to generate let operations.

VarThe variable to find LetClauses for
