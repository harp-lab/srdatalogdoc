Detailed Description

template<CProgram Program, typename RelationsTuple>
struct SRDatalog::AllIndicesFromProgram< Program, RelationsTuple >

Computes and builds all indexes required by a Program.

Analyzes a Program ADT to determine all required indexes and provides a method to build them on a runtime relations tuple.
